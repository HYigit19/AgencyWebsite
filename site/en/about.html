About devunoxAI

We build AI automation, websites, custom software and dashboards for small and mid-sized businesses. With the experience of large enterprise projects, but as simple and fast as a growing company needs.

Who is behind devunoxAI

devunoxAI is a brand of DEVUNOX SOLUTIONS Sp. z o.o., based in Warsaw, Poland. The company was founded and is led by Hüseyin Yigit. He has worked with enterprise software for almost ten years: first as an SAP consultant at Accenture and IBM, and since 2021 independently on projects for industrial and trading companies. His certifications include SAP S/4HANA and a project management certification from PMI.

Those projects taught us two things: how powerful large systems are, and how much manual work is still left over. That is where we come in. We build software that takes over the routine and adapts to your processes, not the other way round.

How we work

  • Free pilot: we build a pilot for your use case at no cost. Only if you say yes do we talk about working together.
  • Fixed price, not hourly billing: after the pilot you receive a binding fixed price.
  • No per-user subscription: your whole team uses the software at no extra cost, whether 5 or 500 people.
  • Your own solution: we build software that belongs to you, not off-the-shelf software with your logo.
  • EU hosting: GDPR compliant, with a data processing agreement. Your data is not used to train AI models.
  • Fast results: you usually see the first clickable prototype after about a week.

Who we work for

Our clients are small and mid-sized businesses in Europe, for example in manufacturing, logistics, trade, property management and services. For public bodies, universities and companies with their own IT, we also offer operation on their own servers.

Our demos show what such solutions can look like. We never sell the demo itself, but always a solution built only for you.
