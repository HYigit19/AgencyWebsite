FAQ

Questions about pricing

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing. We build the pilot solution free of charge and with no obligation: one use case with up to 5 working days of effort, in our test environment. Only once you say yes do we talk about working together – then at the agreed fixed price.

How many revision rounds are included for websites?

Unlimited. We revise the draft until you are happy – within the agreed number of pages.

Why do you only show starting prices?

Every solution is built individually. The price depends on scope, for example integrations, user roles and languages. After the pilot you get a binding fixed price.

What does the fixed price include?

Concept, design, development, testing, setup and onboarding for your team. We discuss extra requests up front and price them transparently.

Does the software cost more if more employees use it?

No. We do not charge per-user licences. For AI solutions, only the model costs depend on actual usage.

Do I have to book operations with you?

No. Hosting and domain can sit with you or with us. With an operations plan, updates, security and support are taken care of.

Where is our data stored and how secure is it?

We host in data centres within the EU and work in line with the GDPR, with a data processing agreement. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is protected by login and role-based permissions. Your data is not used to train AI models.
