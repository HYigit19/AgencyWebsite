AI inbox: requests that almost answer themselves

Every day, enquiries, orders and follow-up questions land in your inbox. The AI inbox sorts them, recognises the request and drafts the reply. Your team checks and sends.

Request a free pilotView the demo

The problem

Sound familiar?

  • The inbox grows faster than you can reply

    Important requests get lost between newsletters, invoices and follow-ups.

  • The same questions over and over

    Delivery times, prices, documents: your team rewrites similar answers every day.

  • Data is typed in by hand

    Name, company, quantity and date are copied from the email into the CRM or ERP.

The solution

How it works

  • Recognise the request

    The AI classifies every email, for example as an enquiry, order, complaint or invoice, and sets a priority.

  • Take over the data

    Customer, product, quantity and deadline are extracted and transferred to your CRM, ERP or a spreadsheet.

  • Draft the reply

    Every request comes with a draft answer based on your documents. A person checks and approves it.

  • To the right person

    The email goes straight to the person in charge, with a summary and the next step.

  • With your existing inbox

    Your team keeps working in the inbox they know. We agree on the right connection in the intro call.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the AI send replies without a check?

Only if you explicitly want it to. Usually the AI prepares a draft and a person approves it.

Which mailboxes are supported?

We connect common mailboxes such as Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. We agree on the right connection in the intro call.

What happens to our emails?

Processing runs in data centres in the EU, GDPR compliant with a data processing agreement. Your data is not used to train AI models.
